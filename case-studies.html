What every case study shows

02
Layers Implemented
Which layers of the AppearAI stack were implemented, in what order, and over what timeline. The specific work done — without revealing proprietary methodology.
03
Competitive Shift
How the competitive landscape changed — which competitors were displaced from AI recommendations and in which prompt categories the business gained ground.
04
Timeline
How long each layer took to implement and how long before the AAR™ score began to move. Real timelines — not aspirational ones.
05
Platform Breakdown
Which AI platforms showed the strongest movement — ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude AI, or Google AI — and which prompts drove the most appearances.
06
Business Context
Category, city, starting conditions, and what the business had tried before. Enough context to understand whether the result is relevant to your situation.
