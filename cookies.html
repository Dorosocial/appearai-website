What are cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. This policy explains how AppearAI uses them.

Essential cookies

Our website works without login and stores very little on your device. Any essential cookies are used only to make the site function properly.

Third-party services

Some features rely on third-party services that may set their own cookies, such as Google Fonts for typography and Formspree for the contact form. These services operate under their own privacy and cookie policies.

Cookies we do not use

We do not use advertising cookies, tracking cookies, or any cookies that build a profile of your browsing across other websites.

Managing cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you block or delete them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.
