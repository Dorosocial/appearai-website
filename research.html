The AAR™ Methodology

Why we measure the way we measure

AI is probabilistic. The same query to ChatGPT returns different answers on different runs — sometimes your business appears, sometimes it doesn't. A single query, run once, tells you almost nothing reliable about your AI visibility.

The AAR™ methodology was designed to produce statistically reliable, comparable results. We run a set of carefully crafted discovery prompts — phrased as real customers would ask them — multiple times each, across multiple AI engines. The number of prompts, runs, and engines scales with the engagement tier: a standard audit begins at 300 evaluations, while advanced tiers run thousands. That sample size produces a meaningful, trackable percentage — and a baseline that allows us to measure improvement over time.

The result is the AI Appearance Rate™: a single number that tells you exactly how often AI recommends your business when someone nearby asks for your service — and a baseline that allows us to measure improvement over time.

Discovery prompts — scaled to your tier
Conversational queries phrased as real customers ask AI — not keyword strings. The number of prompts scales with the engagement tier, starting at 20 and increasing for more comprehensive audits.
Multiple runs per prompt
Each prompt is run multiple times on each engine to account for AI's probabilistic nature. Run count increases with the engagement tier — ensuring appearance rate reflects genuine recommendation probability at the required confidence level.
5 AI engines
ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Claude AI, and Google AI — the platforms accounting for the overwhelming majority of AI search activity in 2026.
