L2

L3

L5

L6
AI Visibility Management — Does it hold and compound over time?
The AAR™ Measurement Framework
How to measure AI visibility reliably. Because AI is probabilistic, a single query run once tells you nothing meaningful. The AAR™ framework is designed to produce statistically reliable, comparable results across time.
01
Write discovery prompts as conversational questions — never keyword strings. "Who is the best dentist near downtown Toronto?" not "top dentist Toronto."
02
Run each prompt multiple times on each engine — run count scales with your required confidence level and engagement tier.
03
Record appearances across all engines. AAR™ = appearances ÷ total evaluations × 100.
04
Run the same battery monthly. The trend line is more valuable than any single score.
05
Benchmark against competitors using the same prompt set. Relative position matters as much as absolute score.
The AI Visibility Audit Framework
How we approach an AI visibility audit — the sequence of checks that builds a complete picture of why a business is or isn't being recommended by AI.
01
Technical audit — HTTPS, schema, crawler access, llms.txt, meta signals, content structure.
02
Entity audit — Knowledge Graph status, NAP consistency, Entity Home strength, cross-platform alignment.
03
Discovery prompt battery — AAR™ calculated across prompts, engines, and runs.
04
Competitor analysis — Who is appearing instead, with what signals, and what the gap looks like.
05
Prioritised fix plan — Actions ranked by expected impact on AAR™, specific to the business.

Glossary

Key terms

AAR™
AI Appearance Rate™ — the percentage of AI evaluation runs in which your business is recommended. Calculated as appearances ÷ total evaluations × 100. Total evaluations scale with the engagement tier — starting at 300 (20 prompts × multiple runs × 5 engines) for a standard audit.
GEO
Generative Engine Optimisation — the discipline of optimising your content and web presence to appear in AI-generated answers. Distinct from traditional SEO which targets search rankings.
Entity
A structured representation of a real-world thing — a business, person, or organisation — as understood by AI platforms. Strong entities are well-defined, consistently represented, and corroborated across multiple sources.
Entity Home
The primary source that AI platforms treat as the authoritative reference for a business. Establishing a strong Entity Home means AI knows where to go for the definitive description of your business.
llms.txt
An emerging standard file placed at your domain root that provides AI language models with a structured guide to your website — what you do, which pages are important, and how to reference you. The AI equivalent of robots.txt.
LLM Seeding
The practice of strategically placing brand mentions, expert citations, and entity references in the high-authority sources that AI language models draw from during training — creating visibility at the foundational level.
Citation Share
The proportion of AI-generated answers in which your business is cited, relative to the total answers generated for queries in your category and location. The AI equivalent of search market share.
Schema Markup
Structured data code added to your website that communicates specific information about your business to AI crawlers in a standardised, machine-readable format.
AI Overview
Google's AI-generated summary that appears above traditional search results for many queries. Being cited in AI Overviews is one of the highest-visibility positions in modern search.
Knowledge Graph
Google's database of entities and their relationships — one of the most influential sources AI platforms draw from when forming their understanding of a business. Appearing accurately in the Knowledge Graph is a significant AI recommendation signal.
