How We Work

Four layers of work. Each one builds on the last. Together they make AI recommend you.

Layer 01
AI Readiness
The launch pad

AI platforms can only recommend what they can read. Most local business websites were built for a different era — one where search engines were the audience. The signals that tell AI who you are, what you do, and where you operate are either missing, incomplete, or actively blocking the very crawlers that would recommend you.

Layer 1 is where every client starts — and where the transformation begins. We implement the technical infrastructure that makes your website readable, structured, and trustworthy to every major AI platform. This is not maintenance work. It is the launch pad everything else is built on.

What changes
AI platforms can find, read, and understand your business. The foundation is set — everything that follows builds directly on this.
Layer 02
Entity Dominance
Making AI know who you are

Being technically readable is the floor. AI platforms understand the world through entities — structured representations of who businesses are, built from interconnected signals. A business without a strong entity is one AI describes vaguely, attributes inconsistently, and recommends hesitantly.

Layer 2 establishes and dominates your entity. We ensure your business is the authoritative, well-defined presence AI reaches for with confidence — described accurately, positioned correctly within your category, and associated with the right signals of trust and expertise in your area.

What changes
AI knows exactly who your business is and why it should be recommended — described accurately and consistently across every platform.
Layer 03
Search & AI Discovery
Found in both worlds

Search and AI are converging. Google's AI Overviews, Perplexity's cited answers, and traditional organic rankings all draw from overlapping signals. Businesses that optimise for one while ignoring the other are leaving significant visibility on the table.

Layer 3 positions your business to be discovered across both channels simultaneously — earning citations in AI-generated answers while strengthening your organic search presence. We track your citation share in AI Overviews and Perplexity monthly, so you can see exactly where you are appearing and where competitors are gaining ground.

What changes
Your business is found whether someone searches traditionally or asks AI — with citation share tracked across both channels and reported monthly.
Layer 04
GEO & Authority Building
Building real authority

AI platforms triangulate authority from a web of external signals. A business that only exists on its own website is one AI recommends cautiously. The businesses AI recommends consistently have corroborating signals across the wider web — signals that confirm the entity is real, established, and trusted in its community.

Layer 4 builds those off-site authority signals strategically. We establish your business across the sources AI platforms use to confirm credibility — community platforms, authoritative references, and brand signals that compound over time and become increasingly difficult for competitors to replicate.

What changes
Your business is corroborated across the web — giving AI the external confirmation it needs to recommend you consistently and without hesitation.
Layer 05
LLM Seeding
The big play

The most durable AI visibility comes from presence in the sources that AI platforms learn from. Businesses that appear in high-authority, widely-cited sources hold a structural advantage — one that compounds over time and is extraordinarily difficult for competitors to close.

Layer 5 builds that presence strategically. We place your business in the sources that shape what AI knows — so that your name, your reputation, and your category leadership are embedded at the level where AI forms its understanding, not just where it searches for answers.

What changes
Your business is present in the sources AI learns from — creating a structural visibility advantage that compounds continuously and is built to last.
Layer 06
AI Visibility Management
The permanent layer

AI is not static. The platforms update their models, new competitors enter your space, and what was working six months ago may need reinforcing today. The businesses that stay visible are not the ones that did the work once — they are the ones that never stopped.

Layer 6 is the ongoing management layer that protects and grows everything built in Layers 1–5. Your AAR™ is tracked monthly, competitive movements are caught early, and every significant platform update is assessed for impact on your visibility — with a response in place before you notice a difference.

What changes
Your AI visibility compounds rather than drifts. Every layer of work is protected, every platform shift is caught, and your position strengthens continuously.
