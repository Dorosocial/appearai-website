The Brand Problem

AI search is not neutral. When someone asks an AI platform about your category, your competitors, or your brand directly, the answer they receive is shaped by a set of signals your brand either controls or doesn't.

Most brands — even large ones — have left this entirely to chance. No entity strategy. No AI citation monitoring. No presence in the sources that LLMs draw from. The result is a brand that exists in AI's world as an incomplete, sometimes inaccurate, often underrepresented version of itself.

Layers 3 and 4 of our work address this at the level of brand authority and AI training data — work that requires deep expertise and cannot be replicated by an SEO agency running AI tools.

The Work

Four layers. Built in sequence. The result is a brand AI trusts, cites, and recommends.

Layer 01
AI Readiness
The launch pad

Before any visibility work can have full effect, the technical infrastructure must be in place. AI platforms index, read, and evaluate your web presence constantly — and the signals that communicate what your brand is, what it stands for, and who it serves must be present, accurate, and unobstructed.

Layer 1 is the launch pad. We implement the technical standards that make your brand readable and trustworthy to every major AI platform — creating the conditions for every subsequent layer to work at full power.

What changes
Your brand is technically sound. AI platforms can read you accurately and completely — and every layer of work that follows has the foundation it needs to deliver full impact.
Layer 02
Entity Dominance
Making AI know who you are

A brand without a strong entity is a brand AI represents incompletely. Layer 2 builds and dominates your entity — optimising your Knowledge Graph presence, establishing your Entity Home, and building the semantic content clusters that tell AI exactly who you are, what you stand for, and why you lead your category.

This is the work that turns a brand from something AI knows about into something AI understands deeply.

What changes
Your brand's entity is established and dominant — AI describes you accurately, positions you correctly, and recommends you with confidence.
Layer 03
Search & AI Discovery
Found in both worlds

Layer 3 addresses both traditional search and AI-generated discovery simultaneously. We implement the content architecture and optimisation that earns citations in AI Overviews and Perplexity while compounding your organic search authority.

Citation share in AI-generated answers is tracked monthly across the major platforms — giving you a clear, measurable view of your AI market position relative to competitors.

What changes
Your brand appears across both traditional search and AI-generated answers — with monthly citation tracking that shows exactly where you stand.
Layer 04
GEO & Authority Building
Building real authority

Authority in AI's world is not self-declared — it is corroborated. Layer 4 builds the off-site signals that confirm your brand's authority across the sources AI platforms draw from: community platforms, niche publications, authoritative references, and the brand signals that establish you as a genuine leader in your category.

This is compounding work. Every signal added in Layer 4 strengthens the ones built before it — creating a web of authority that grows more resilient and more valuable over time.

What changes
Your brand's authority is corroborated across the web — with the external signals that give AI the confidence to recommend you above alternatives.
Layer 05
LLM Seeding
The big play

The most sophisticated layer of AI visibility work operates at the level of what AI platforms are trained on. Brands that appear consistently in the high-authority sources that shape LLM training data hold a structural advantage that is extraordinarily difficult for competitors to replicate.

Layer 5 places your brand in those sources — strategically and authentically — so that your name, your positioning, and your category authority are embedded in AI's understanding at the foundational level.

What changes
Your brand is present in the sources AI learns from — a structural advantage that makes your visibility self-reinforcing and increasingly hard to compete with.
Layer 06
AI Visibility Management
The permanent layer

AI platforms update continuously. Models are retrained, citation sources shift, and competitive positions change — sometimes significantly. The brands that hold their AI visibility are not those that built it once. They are the ones that never stopped managing it.

Layer 6 is the permanent management layer. Your brand's visibility is monitored continuously — AAR™ tracked across platforms, share of voice measured monthly, model updates assessed for impact, and every signal maintained to the standard set by the work beneath it.

What changes
Your brand's AI visibility is a managed, living asset — monitored continuously, protected proactively, and built to compound in your favour month after month.
