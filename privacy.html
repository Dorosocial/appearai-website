1. Who we are

AppearAI is a product of Avarabrands Inc., registered in Delaware, United States. Our registered address is 131 Continental Dr, Suite 305, Newark, Delaware 19713. You can contact us at hello@appearai.io with any questions about this policy.

2. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly to us — including your name, email address, business name, website URL, city, and business category when you use our scanner or purchase an audit. We also collect information automatically when you visit our website, including IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent on pages. If you book a call via Calendly, Calendly processes your booking information under their own privacy policy.

3. How we use your information

We use the information we collect to deliver your AI Visibility Audit, send you your PDF report, schedule your walkthrough call, improve our services and methodology, and send you relevant communications about AI visibility (which you can unsubscribe from at any time). We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Email communications

When you submit your email via our scanner or audit purchase, you may receive communications from us about your results and our services. Every email we send includes an unsubscribe link. We use Formspree to process email submissions from our website.

5. Payment processing

Payments for the Full AI Visibility Audit are processed by Stripe. We do not store your payment card information. Stripe's privacy policy governs the processing of your payment data. You can review Stripe's privacy policy at stripe.com/privacy.

6. Data retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to deliver our services and comply with our legal obligations. Audit reports and associated business data are retained for 24 months. You may request deletion of your data at any time by emailing hello@appearai.io.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal information. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@appearai.io. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies

Our website uses essential cookies for functionality and analytics cookies to understand how visitors use our site. You can manage your cookie preferences via our Cookie Policy page. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by email if you have provided your email address. The date at the top of this policy indicates when it was last updated.

10. Contact

For any privacy-related questions or requests, email hello@appearai.io. For formal correspondence: Avarabrands Inc., 131 Continental Dr, Suite 305, Newark, Delaware 19713, United States.
