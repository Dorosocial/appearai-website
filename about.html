The AAR™ origin

Why we invented
the AI Appearance Rate™

When we started working on AI visibility for local businesses, we immediately ran into the same problem: nobody could agree on what to measure. Rankings don't exist in AI. Traffic attribution is impossible. Impressions are unverifiable.

So we designed a measurement methodology from first principles. AI is probabilistic — the same query returns different answers on different runs. The only way to measure AI visibility reliably is to run many prompts, many times, across many engines — and count appearances.

That is the AAR™. 20 prompts × 3 runs × 5 engines = 300 evaluations. Appearances ÷ 300 × 100 = your AI Appearance Rate™. A single, comparable, trackable number that tells you exactly where you stand.

The formula
Appearances ÷ 300 × 100
